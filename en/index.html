About Us

Gonzalez Corona, Chapa, Covarrubias y Asociados S. C. is a firm made up of expert attorneys in different areas of law, brought together to achieve a common objective: to provide high-quality comprehensive legal advice to all types of companies. The team is composed of attorneys who graduated from the most prestigious universities, dynamic, entrepreneurial, highly qualified, up to date, and broadly experienced in different branches of law.
